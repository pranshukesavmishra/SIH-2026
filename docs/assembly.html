Before → after is our own fix, both measured with the full specified disturbance present. A constant-velocity platform drift is a ramp, and a PD loop has finite steady-state error to a ramp — wrong loop order, not bad tuning. A second integrator cut acquisition 7× and median error 14×. All three rows are still open against the spec and the table says so.

Switch type-2 loop off to watch the ramp reassert itself: the error stops converging and parks at a constant offset. That is the original bug, reproduced live.

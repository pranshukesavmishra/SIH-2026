Live tracking, in the browser.

Our Live demo page on a standard laptop webcam. No special sensor, no install: the whole engine runs on the page.

  1. 01
    Detect. Every bright point in the frame is found and followed.
  2. 02
    Identify. Only a light blinking at 4.0 Hz is accepted. Lamps, screens and glints are rejected.
  3. 03
    Lock and track. The lock follows the beacon and reports its offset from the aim point.
  4. 04
    Real time. 8–21 ms of processing inside each 33 ms frame.

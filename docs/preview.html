WHAT CONNECTS TO WHAT

Laptop USB 1rig Arduino Nano (commands out, angles back)
Laptop USB 2webcam on the head (the video)
Wall socketextension board → 12 V adapter (rig motors only) + laptop charger
Beaconits own power bank · NOT connected to the laptop
Decoyits own 3 AA cells · NOT connected to anything
Red LEDsbeacon AND decoy: the same kind of red LED
White LEDoptional: a 3rd steady light to show it is ignored too

Only 2 USB cables go to the laptop. A laptop with only USB-C needs a small hub. The beacon talks to the rig through the camera: its blink is the message.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

ZeroDrift PAT System: Operation and Test

How to run, verify and shut down the ZeroDrift pointing, acquisition and tracking (PAT) system for free-space optical communication: the simulation test in the Console, live optical tracking, and the MK2 stepper terminal.

DOCUMENT NO.ZD-SOP-001

1Purpose

This procedure makes every test and demonstration of ZeroDrift repeatable. Any team member following it should get the same results, recorded in the same format, whichever system is being run.

2Scope

  • Simulation test: replay of logged engine runs in the Console, with the automatic test record (Section 7).
  • Live optical tracking: detection and tracking of a 4 Hz beacon with a webcam in the browser (Section 8).
  • MK2 terminal: the two-axis stepper gimbal with laser and webcam, driven from the browser (Section 9).

Out of scope: fine-stage (fast steering mirror) control, and link budget analysis. Those are covered in the technical report.

3Definitions

TERMMEANING
PATPointing, acquisition and tracking: finding the far terminal and keeping the optical link aimed at it.
BeaconThe light source to be tracked. It blinks at a known rate (4 Hz), which identifies it.
DecoyAny other light in view: steady lamps, reflections, sun glints, a steady red LED used in tests. It must never be locked.
BoresightThe centre of the camera view, where the terminal is pointing.
Pointing errorAngle between boresight and beacon, in µrad (1 µrad ≈ 1 mm at 1 km).
FOVField of view of the camera. Simulation: 6° (±52,360 µrad).
VrefReference voltage on each A4988 driver that sets the motor current limit.

Tracker states, in the order they normally occur:

SEARCH→ACQUIRE→TRACK→COAST→REACQUIRE→ back to TRACK

COAST means the beacon is briefly dark (off-phase of the blink or short occlusion) and the tracker holds its prediction. This is normal.

6Safety

⚠ LASERNever look into the laser or point it at people, mirrors or glossy surfaces. Keep the laser off (L0) unless a test step needs it.
⚠ ELECTRICAL12 V goes only to the motor rail and the drivers' VMOT pins. It must never reach the 5 V rail, the Arduino, VDD or the sensors. Wire only with everything unplugged. Never connect or disconnect a motor while 12 V is on.
⚠ MECHANICALKeep hands and cables clear of the moving head. Stop immediately with the kill switch on any grinding, smell, smoke or hot component.
POWER ORDERPower on: USB first, then 12 V. Power off: 12 V first, then USB.

7Procedure A: simulation test (Console)

Replays a logged run of the tracking engine and produces a test record automatically. No hardware is needed.

  1. Open Console in a desktop browser.The viewport shows the scenario and the play button shows ❚❚ (playing).
  2. Select a scenario: Decoy field (3 moving decoys and glints, 130 s), ISS pass over Bengaluru (45 s, SGP4 orbit) or Hard turbulence (400 µrad tilt, 30 s).
  3. Let the run play to the end. Watch the state move SEARCH → ACQUIRE → TRACK, and the pointing error fall.The green brackets stay on the beacon; red ◇ decoys are never bracketed.
  4. At the end the debrief opens by itself (or press ▤). Read the Test record at the bottom: nine test cases, each with its criterion, measured value and PASS / FAIL.
  5. Record the test record ID (for example ZD-SIM-DECOYS), the score and the date in the log (Section 13). Repeat A2–A5 for each scenario.
REGENERATING THE LOGSThe engine is open source. pip install -e ".[gui,dev]", then pytest to run the automated test suite and python -m fsoc_pat.gui.app for the desktop GUI.

8Procedure B: live optical tracking (browser)

  1. Dim the room. Switch the beacon on (4 Hz) and place the steady decoy in the same view.
  2. Open Live Demo and press START CAMERA; allow camera access.Status shows SEARCHING and the frame rate is 25 fps or more.
  3. Bring the beacon into view.Within about 2 s the status turns LOCKED, the lock box sits on the beacon and the blink rate reads 4.0 Hz.
  4. Move the beacon slowly, then quickly, across the whole view.The lock follows; offset AZ / EL and target speed update live. The decoy is never locked.
  5. Cover the beacon for 1 s, then uncover it.The tracker coasts, then relocks without a manual reset.
  6. For a red LED beacon in a bright room, turn on RED BEACON FILTER in Settings.

9Procedure C: MK2 terminal

C.1Pre-power checks (everything unplugged)

  1. Beep-test the rails: 12 V rail to 5 V rail must not beep; 12 V rail to GND must not beep (a short chirp from the capacitor is normal).
  2. Check each A4988: VMOT → 12 V, VDD → 5 V, EN → GND, MS1–MS3 → 5 V, RST joined to SLP, 100 µF across VMOT / GND with the stripe to GND.
  3. Check signals: pan STEP / DIR on D2 / D3, tilt STEP / DIR on D4 / D5, laser on D7, sensor bus on A4 / A5 through the multiplexer.
  4. Turn both axes by hand through their full range: no cable goes tight.

C.2Power-up and commissioning

  1. Kill switch OFF. Connect the Nano USB to the laptop.The Nano power LED is on; 5 V rail reads about 5 V.
  2. First run only: set Vref to 0.55 V on both drivers (black probe GND, red probe on the trimmer, USB power only).
  3. Flash the firmware if needed (rig_firmware_v2.ino, 115200 baud).The serial ready line appears; it reports the encoders it found.
  4. Safety officer switches 12 V ON.Both motor shafts hold stiff; nothing gets hot.
  5. Motion check over serial: ? status, P200 T0 (pan about 22.5°), P0 T100 (tilt about 11°), C back to centre.Each axis moves in the commanded direction and returns to centre. With the AS5600 fitted, ? after a move reports the same pan steps as commanded.

C.3Closed-loop tracking

  1. In Live Demo press CONNECT RIG, choose the Nano's port, then MK2 CAMERA (USB) and TEST MOTION.
  2. Bring the beacon into view.The head turns until the beacon is on boresight and follows it as it moves; the decoy is ignored.
  3. Soft limits are pan ±90° and tilt ±18°. The terminal must stop at the limits and never strike an end stop.

10Shutdown

  1. Stop tracking; send C to centre the head and L0 for laser off.
  2. Kill switch OFF (12 V off), then unplug the USB.
  3. Unplug the 12 V adapter from the wall. Switch off the beacon and decoy.

11Acceptance criteria

The simulation test record (Section 7) grades every run against the same fixed criteria. A run passes when all graded cases pass.

IDPARAMETERCRITERION
TC-01Acquisition time, cold start to first lock≤ 3.0 s
TC-02Lock retention after first lock≥ 90 %
TC-03Median pointing error≤ 2 % of FOV half-width
TC-0495th-percentile pointing error≤ 5 % of FOV half-width
TC-05Beacon kept in field of view≥ 95 % of frames
TC-06Frames locked on a decoy≤ 1 % of frames
TC-0795th-percentile compute time per frame≤ one frame period (33.3 ms at 30 Hz)
TC-08Frames over the real-time budget≤ 1 % of frames
TC-09Re-acquisitionsRecorded, not graded

Live and MK2 runs pass when every ✓ VERIFY condition in Sections 8 and 9 is met.

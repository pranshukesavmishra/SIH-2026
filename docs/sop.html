Tracker states

WHAT THE STATUS LIGHT MEANS

SEARCHSpiral scan, widens until something is seen
ACQUIREBeacon identified, steering onto it
TRACKLocked; error measured on the image
COASTBeacon dark (blink / cloud): follow the prediction. Normal
REACQUIRESmall spiral at the last good position

5

Safety

READ BEFORE PROCEDURE C

Laser

Never at eyes, people or mirrors. Off (L0) unless needed.

Electrical

12 V only to the motor rail. Wire unplugged. No motor swaps while powered.

Moving head

Hands and cables clear. Kill switch on any noise, smell or heat.

POWER ON
① USB→② 12 V switch ON
POWER OFF
① 12 V switch OFF→② USB out

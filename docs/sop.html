How our software decides

Every 33 milliseconds our engine runs this loop once. Every box is real code in src/fsoc_pat/pipeline.py, and every YES / NO is a decision it really makes.

PROOFIt follows the 4 Hz blink, not the brightest light
Plotted from our logged decoy-field run: the light we lock on measures 4.01 Hz for all 130 seconds. A steady lamp reads 0 Hz, so however bright it is, it can never win.
PROOF64 passes, 0 wrong-light locks
Every bar is one real run of our Monte-Carlo campaign, with 900 stars in view. All 64 locked; none ever locked a wrong light. Three runs were slower than 3 s: we show them, we do not hide them.

What the status light means

Press play and watch the label on the lock box. It goes SEARCH → ACQUIRE → TRACK, and flickers to COAST every time the beacon blinks off.

PROOFOur unedited laptop-webcam recording
Nothing here is edited or simulated: a real LED, a real webcam, our real software.
SEARCHNothing locked yet. We sweep a growing spiral until a light shows up.
ACQUIREWe found the 4 Hz blink. Now we steer onto it.
TRACKLocked. We measure the error on the picture itself.
COASTThe beacon blinked off. We keep following our prediction. This is normal.
REACQUIRELost for too long. A small spiral where we last saw it.

Safety first

We follow these three rules every single time we switch MK2 on. One of us always keeps a hand on the 12 V switch, so everything can be stopped in one second.

The laser

We never point it at eyes, people or mirrors. It stays off (L0) until a step needs it.

The 12 volts

12 V goes only to the motor drivers. We wire with everything unplugged, and never swap a motor while it is powered.

The moving head

We keep hands and cables clear, and switch off at any grinding, smell or heat.

How we power on
① USB→② 12 V switch ON
The Arduino wakes up first, so it is in control before the motors get any power.
How we power off
① 12 V switch OFF→② USB out
The motors lose power first, so nothing can move by surprise.
PROOFOur real wiring sheet: where the 12 V can and cannot go
The sheet we wire MK2 from: 22 numbered wires, 12 V (red) only on the driver VMOT pins. Tap to open it full size.

What we check first when something is off.

SYMPTOMLIKELY CAUSEFIX

Locks a lampFlicker near 4 Hz · strong reflectionTake it out of view · keep windows out of frame
Motor only buzzesCoil pair split across 1A/1B and 2A/2BRe-pair with the meter (2–4 Ω per pair)

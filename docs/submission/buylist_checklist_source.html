DO NOT BUY THE OV7670. It is a parallel-DVP sensor, not a USB camera, and it cannot work here for three independent reasons. (1) No USB — the pipeline runs on the laptop and opens the camera with OpenCV; the OV7670 exposes D0–D7, PCLK, HREF, VSYNC and SCCB, which no laptop can read. (2) The Nano cannot read it either: ~12 MHz pixel clock against a 16 MHz ATmega328P with 2 KB of SRAM, when one 640×480 frame is 307 KB. (3) The bare board has no AL422B FIFO, and even the FIFO version manages a few FPS at 160×120 where the spec needs ≥20 FPS at 640×480.
BUY THIS INSTEAD. Ask for a USB webcam module, UVC, 640×480 or better, M12 lens. The test is simple: plug it into a laptop, and if it appears as an ordinary webcam it will work.
THE MAGNET IS THE ONE THAT LOOKS IDENTICAL. A diametric magnet is polarised across its diameter; an axial magnet is polarised through its thickness. Same cylinder, same size, same price, same silver finish — and an axial magnet spinning in front of an AS5600 presents an unchanging field, so the encoder reads a constant and both feedback axes are dead. Nothing in the build tells you why. Say the word “diametric” out loud at the counter and check the listing text before paying.

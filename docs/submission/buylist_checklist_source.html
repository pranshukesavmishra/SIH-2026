Tools — not part of the ₹6,463, not the build

Not part of the build and not in the total. These are what you need in your hand to assemble it. Tick 'have' for anything the team or the college lab already owns -- most teams own most of this. The multimeter is the one that is genuinely not optional.

MultimeterSetting the A4988 Vref is a voltage measurement on the driver's trim pot. Without it you are guessing the motor current: too low and the gimbal skips steps, too high and the motor cooks. There is no way to do this by eye.₹500
Small flat screwdriver, 2 mmThe A4988 trim pot is tiny and a normal screwdriver slips off it onto the board.₹150
Wire stripper / side cutterThirty-odd connections.₹150
Soldering iron, 25-40 WThe beacon and decoy are soldered; solder itself is already in consumables.₹350

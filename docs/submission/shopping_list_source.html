ZeroDrift MK2: shopping list

Team ZeroDrift · Jabalpur Engineering College · SIH 2026 · for Amar Robotics

For the shopkeeper: we are building a small pan-tilt camera turret with two NEMA17 stepper motors (5 mm shaft) and A4988 drivers, run by an Arduino Nano. We need the items below.

15 mm rigid flange coupling2Metal flange coupling with a 5 mm bore, to fit a NEMA17 stepper motor shaft. Rigid (solid) type, not the flexible or spring type. Must have a grub screw and a flange with 4 screw holes. Sometimes sold as a "5 mm wheel hub / shaft coupler".160–300
